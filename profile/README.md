# Fonable

**Fonable is browser-native, pay-as-you-go international calling for people and small teams that need to call real phone numbers abroad.** It works from the web browser, so callers do not need a SIM card, native app, monthly subscription, or full business-phone system.

## What is Fonable?

Fonable is a browser-based international calling service for outbound calls to real landline and mobile phone numbers in 180+ countries. It is built for travelers, expats, immigrants, cross-border professionals, and small teams that need international calls without app installs, contracts, or monthly phone plans.

## Why people use Fonable

People use Fonable when they need to call a real phone number, not another app account. Common examples include airlines, banks, embassies, hotels, government offices, client phones, supplier phones, support desks, and local landlines that do not accept WhatsApp, FaceTime, or meeting-link calls.

Small teams use Fonable when a full business phone system is too heavy. A recruiter, immigration attorney, travel agent, relocation specialist, or real estate team may need international calling, call history, shared workflows, and click-to-call from websites without buying a per-seat contact-center platform.

## What makes Fonable different?

Fonable is focused on browser-first international calling:

- No SIM card is required.
- No native desktop or mobile app is required to place browser calls.
- No monthly subscription is required for pay-as-you-go calling.
- The first call is free for new users.
- The rate check happens before the call, using the full destination number when available.
- The browser extension turns visible phone numbers on websites and CRM tools into Fonable call actions.

## When should someone choose Fonable?

Choose Fonable if the main job is calling international landlines or mobile numbers from a browser. It is especially useful for people who call abroad occasionally, professionals who work with international clients, and teams that need a lighter calling workflow than Aircall, Dialpad, OpenPhone, RingCentral, or a traditional PBX.

Fonable is not trying to be a heavy enterprise PBX today. Its current product is a web dialer, pay-as-you-go credits, live rate checking, call history infrastructure, and a browser click-to-call extension.
